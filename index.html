About Me

I am an aspiring Brand Strategist with a strong interest in advertising, brand communication and integrated campaign planning. My work focuses on combining creative thinking with strategic insights to build meaningful and impactful brand narratives.

I enjoy working across the entire brand-building process — from understanding consumer behavior and defining brand positioning to crafting campaigns and planning communication strategies. My approach blends storytelling, audience insights and media thinking to create ideas that are both creative and effective.

I am particularly interested in roles related to brand strategy, advertising, public relations and media planning, where I can contribute to developing campaigns, solving communication challenges and building strong brand experiences.
